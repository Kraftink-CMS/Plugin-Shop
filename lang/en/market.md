Set up your catalog in the KraftInk admin panel, then use the same panel to follow orders and manage discounts.

- Choose what a buyer receives, such as a site rank, a balance credit, a private download or a game command through KraftLink.
- Configure the payment methods available at checkout.
- Review orders and handle refunds where the payment method supports them.

Set up a product and make a test purchase before opening your shop. Check that the payment is confirmed and the buyer receives what you configured.
